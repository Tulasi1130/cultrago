About

Telangana is India’s youngest state, established on June 2, 2014, and is located on the semi-arid Deccan Plateau in the south-central region. Known for its distinct Ganga-Jamuna Tehzeeb, the state offers a unique cultural blend of Persian influence and traditional South Indian heritage, primarily expressed through the Telugu and Urdu languages. Its capital, Hyderabad, is a premier global hub for Information Technology, pharmaceuticals, and aerospace, consistently driving the state toward one of the highest per capita incomes in the country. Beyond its modern economic success and high-tech "Future City" initiatives, Telangana is anchored by a rich history—ranging from the architectural legacy of the Kakatiya dynasty to the vibrant floral festivals like Bathukamma—and is geographically defined by the lifelines of the Godavari and Krishna rivers.

⚠️Do’s & Don’ts

✅ Do’s

  • Respect local culture
  • Try local food
  • Follow traditions in temples
  • Download Rapido or Ola for transport
  • Download T-Savaari app for Metro

❌ Don’ts

  • Do not insist on beef or pork
  • Avoid Monday/Friday visits
  • Do not accept the first price in markets
  • Don't wander alone late at night.
  • Do not insult/judge the accent
