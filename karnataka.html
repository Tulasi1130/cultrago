⚠️ Do’s & Don’ts

✅ Do’s

  • Respect historical monuments
  • Explore heritage sites like Hampi
  • Try local Karnataka cuisine

❌ Don’ts

  • Do not damage monuments
  • Avoid littering tourist places
  • Do not disturb wildlife in forest areas

🌤️ Best Time to Visit

October to February is the best time to visit Karnataka due to pleasant weather.
